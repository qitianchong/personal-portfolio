The Facts About Panda

Specialized bears native to southwest China, renowned for their, distinct black-and-white coat, and almost exclusive diet of bamboo. They are actually a large mammals

Fun Fact About Panda

Male pandas are known to perform "handstands" against trees to leave their scent higher up, showcasing their height to rivals. Additionally, pandas are notoriously lazy, eating for 12–16 hours a day and pooping up to 40 times, sometimes without waking up from their naps. They also consume horse manure to keep warm
